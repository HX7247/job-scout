What Job Scout knows about you

How complete is your LinkedIn profile?

Checked against LinkedIn's own profile-strength framework — the same seven sections its Beginner/Intermediate/All-Star meter counts — plus a short, separately-labelled list of Job Scout's own quality checks. Your own profile only; this app never fetches or scores anyone else's.

Who's hiring in your field

Companies with the most early-career postings in your subject, counted from what this install has actually scraped — not LinkedIn alumni data, which nobody publishes an API for and which only LinkedIn itself can show, to someone signed in, browsing it themselves.

Your own skills

The built-in vocabulary knows about three hundred technical terms and will never know your niche instrument, your society, or an in-house tool. Anything you add here is matched and scored exactly like a recognised skill.

Your own filters

A label, some words, and what to do when they appear. Tick “show as a checkbox” and it joins the filters in the sidebar with its own live count.
